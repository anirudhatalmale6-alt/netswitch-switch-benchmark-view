Networks & the MVNOs on Them

Four physical networks carry every SIM in the UK — most value brands ride on them
EE / Three
The two premium-coverage MNOs. Three is the price leader (unlimited £22); EE the priciest (unlimited £40). Smarty & iD Mobile resell Three from £3.90.
Vodafone
Unlimited £26. Hosts Voxi (unlimited £15), Lebara (5GB £5) and ASDA Mobile — value brands on Vodafone masts.
O2 (VMO2)
Entry SIM from £5.80, EU roaming across the range. Hosts Giffgaff and Tesco Mobile; VMO2 closed 2024 with 45.7M connections.
MVNO layer
Sky Mobile + Tesco Mobile alone hold >15% of the consumer segment — the cheapest per-GB data in the market lives here.
