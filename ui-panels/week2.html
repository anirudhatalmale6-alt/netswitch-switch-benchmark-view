Three running signals: Black (noise floor), Grey (mid), White (clean) in dB. The selector is your [X] Low [0] Medium [ ] High — [X] active, [0] available, [ ] off. As throughput falls (1500 → 32 kbps) the switch drops the codec bitrate but protects the call: when White quality dips below target it reroutes to a cleaner path rather than let the call degrade. Lower throughput, better call — that trade is the engine behind this panel.
